## Introduction
The goal of this project was to decipher which was more important when predicting the success of a movie, the director or the studio. The variables that were used to measure success were the average ratings and the popularity of the movie. The data was collected from the TMDB data base.

## Write Up
After looking at the data through our visualizations there are many things than we can conclude. First we can conclude that neither director or studio has a significant impact on the ratings a movie receives. When looking at the bar charts, there are minimal differences in ratings between the directors and the studios. On the other hand popularity does seem to be a significant variable. When looking at the directors bar chart the popularity between director seems to be relatively consistent. you do have a large jump with directors like Christopher Nolan but besides that they seem to be pretty equal. Now looking at the Studio's bar chart we can see a lot of disparity. Some studios see a high amount of popularity while others have very low popularity. A conclusion we can draw from this data is that large scale directors are often met with more consistent popularity for their movies when compared to large scale studios. 

## Limitations
When collecting data from this API I ran into many complication with collecting data. First of when pulling data I wasn't able to pull more than just the Most popular movies. This could definitely lead to skewed data. Also when only comparing directors and studios I didn't have any variables that could differentiate genre which could have led to more niche genres (which a studio or director may specialize in) being under represented. If I were to do this project again i would try and gather more confounding variable to get a better idea of how to measure success when it comes to movies.
