## Blog 1
This blog post is an introduction to my Data Science career. I find data science such a unique career path because data can be anything. whether it be the points from a basketball game or the known diseases in the world, all of them are considered data. this course in particular offers a unique opportunity that I wouldn't be able to experience without it. Particularly when it comes to group projects, there is so much to learn about and working as a team gives us real world experience. personally, I hope this course can teach me how to make use of data that doesn't have an implied use. For example, I am hoping to do a research project on how different biomechanical advantages can help in certain sports. The goal of this research project is to create the perfect athlete for 15 different sports. the biggest hurdle that I believe this class could help with is biomechanics for professional athletes are private and hard to get a hold of without the consent. even with that in mind there are ways to calculate the rough estimates of different biomechanical advantages. I'm hoping this course can help me correlate public information and statistics to calculate rough estimates for many different biomechanics. In the first studio class I took has year I felt that our data collection was starting to trend in the direction of understanding data past just numbers on a computer. In studio 2 I'm hoping that this trend continues and I am able to further my knowledge of comprehending data.
